# D-123 Resume bullet 2 cites measurement 8, not measurement 4's A and B

- **Decision:** The owner's final text for the definition of done (PLAN.md and CLAUDE.md, 2026-11-14) replaces bullet 2's template ("under k6 load with [X]% injected provider failures, raised successful responses from [A]% to [B]% at [N] req/s, with [Y] ms p95 gateway overhead") with: "Added timeouts, jittered retries, a circuit breaker, and pre-first-byte provider fallback; in k6 load tests, served 100% of requests through a 30 s full outage of one provider (0% without fallback), with the gateway adding 2.0 ms at p95." Bullet 1 is unchanged. The numbers trace to docs/RESULTS.md: 30 s, 100% and 0% to measurement 8's "Inside the window" row (fallback on 100.00% and off 0.00%, in all three runs of each); 2.0 ms to measurement 1's p95 overhead, 2.02 ms [1.81 to 2.11]. The template's mapping line ("X, A, B and N come from measurement 4") is removed.
- **Why:** Measurement 4's A is not a gateway without its reliability layer. Retries, timeouts and the breaker have no switch (D-050), so A has them on and only fallback is off. With up to 3 attempts at an independent 30% failure rate, a request fails only if all three attempts do, 1 − 0.3³ = 97.3% succeed, and A measured 97.20%. "From 97.2% to 100%" would therefore credit the layer with 2.8 points when most of its effect is already inside A. Measurement 8 isolates a case the layer exists for: one provider fully down, where without fallback nothing succeeds for 30 s and with it everything does.
- **Alternatives:** The template filled from measurement 4: "30% injected failures, from 97.2% to 100% at 50 req/s" (accurate, but it understates the layer and reads as a small effect). A bare-call baseline for A: it needs a D-050 amendment to add a switch for retries and was not run, so no number for it exists.
- **Kept as reported:** Measurement 4 stays in RESULTS.md exactly as measured: A 97.20% [96.87 to 97.40], B 100.00% [100.00 to 100.00], the 2.8-point difference, and the 0.3³ = 2.7% analysis showing that 97.2% matches the retry model. Nothing in it is changed or hidden by this choice.
- **Risk:** "100% through a 30 s full outage" holds for these conditions: the secondary is healthy, failures are fast injected errors, one laptop, mocks, 50 req/s (RESULTS.md, Known limitations). The 2.0 ms overhead was measured without injected failures. D-121's risk line expected the bullet to state the 50 req/s rate; the owner's text does not, so the rate is stated in RESULTS.md and in the trace lines under the bullets.
- **Revisit when:** A bare-call baseline is run (after a D-050 amendment), or the measurements are repeated on other hardware or against real providers.
